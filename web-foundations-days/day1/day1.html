About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking application that helps you capture and organize your ideas in one place. It is designed to make it easy to record important thoughts, reminders, and tasks whenever you need them.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page to access your notes.
  2. Find the "Add a note" section.
  3. Type your idea or reminder into the text box.
  4. Click the "Add Note" button to submit your note.
  5. View your notes in the "Your notes" section.

Features

  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • View a list of your notes.
  • See how many notes you have.
  • Navigate between the Home and About pages.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Tab Move to the next interactive element.
